## 1.3 Critique method

Each use case is critiqued against the case-study requirements in two parts:

- **Functional design (90%).** Requirement coverage, logical soundness and UML correctness of the use case, class and sequence diagrams and the scenario.
- **Interaction design (10%).** Usability, logical flow and adherence to HCI principles (Nielsen's ten usability heuristics) in the original wireframes and hi-fi screens.

Every weakness gets an ID, `W<chapter>.<n>` (for example `W3.4`), and a severity of **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. Every proposed change cites the weakness IDs it fixes, so each improvement can be traced back to the critique. No change is made without a cited weakness.
